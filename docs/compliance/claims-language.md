# Claims language — clinical decision support (CDS)

**For:** Landing, demos, pilot proposals, investor one-liners.  
**Review:** Part-time clinical advisor (EM / urgent care India) before wide outbound.

## Allowed (preferred)

- “AI-assisted triage support with clinician Confirm / Override”
- “Structured intake, live queue, and audit trail”
- “Decision support — not a diagnosis”
- “Urgency suggestion is advisory until reviewed”
- “Built for Indian acute-care desks / ED walk-in”

## Avoid

- “Autonomous ER AI” / “replaces nurses” / “replaces triage”
- “Diagnoses patients” / “prescribes”
- Unqualified “accuracy %” or “reduces mortality” without a measurement protocol and advisor sign-off
- “ESI certified” or claiming local protocol certification without rights

## Product chrome (already in app)

Persistent banner: AI decision support — clinician must confirm before action.

## One-line pitch (from plan)

“Auditable AI-assisted triage for Indian acute care — structured intake, live queue, clinician confirmation — sold site-by-site with measured pilot proof.”
